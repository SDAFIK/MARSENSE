Note to Users Regarding Mars 2020 Bundles
The Mars 2020 data archive was reconfigured into a new set of bundles, after the original Release 1. Whereas, the data was divided only by instrument before, it is now divided by instrument and data type. To access the original, superseded Release 1, click HERE.
